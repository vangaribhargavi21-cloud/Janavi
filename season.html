Seasons of the Year

Experience the beauty of nature in every season 🌍

Summer ☀️

Summer is the hottest season of the year. The sun shines brightly and the days are long. People enjoy cold drinks, fruits, and ice creams during this time.

It is also the season of holidays, where children travel and enjoy their vacations happily.

Rainy Season 🌧️

The rainy season brings cool weather and fresh air. Trees and plants become green and lively.

Children enjoy playing in rain and making paper boats. This season is very helpful for farmers.

Winter ❄️

Winter is the coldest season. The weather becomes cool and pleasant.

People wear warm clothes like sweaters and jackets and enjoy hot food and drinks.

Spring 🌸

Spring is the most beautiful season. Flowers bloom and nature becomes colorful.

The weather is pleasant and it represents happiness and new beginnings.
